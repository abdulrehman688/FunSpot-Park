Business Opportunities





FunZone Amusement Park offers various business opportunities within the Park system. Examples include restaurant and hotel management, golf pro shops, souvenir and campstore sales, hot air ballooning, white water rafting, firewood sales and more. These and other types of business opportunities are available in FunZone Park.

FunZone Park encourages new and innovative ideas for serving our patrons that are consistent with our image and mission. Inquiries are welcome. Business partners are generally selected through a public and competitive bidding process.

Individuals or corporations that are interested in being added to our mailing list for new opportunities can contact the Concessions Management Bureau by funzone@gmail.com or by fax at +923406886905. Please be sure to include your name, complete mailing address, phone number, fax number and e-mail address on all correspondence as well as the types of businesses and locations (counties) that you are interested in.
